Writing (05)

I write to make sense of the work— what happens when images leave the screen and become rooms, weather and light.

These are essays and field notes on designing for physical space: data, generative systems, motion, and the slow, practical craft of building things that live in public. Writing is how I slow down enough to see what I actually made.
