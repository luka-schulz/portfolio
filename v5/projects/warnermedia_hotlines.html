Warner Media Hotlines

Installation R&D

Hotlines is an interactive installation located in the Warner Media HQ. Rare Volume brought me on to do R&D and look development for the installation. A combination of Houdini and Python were used to ingest movie scripts and feed them into smoke and particle simulations.
