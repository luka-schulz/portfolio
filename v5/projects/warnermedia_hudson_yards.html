WarnerMedia—Hudson Yards

Generative Art

Rare Volume designed and engineered the media experience for WarnerMedia’s Hudson Yards Headquarters. The experience consists of 6 touch points spread across 11 stories, intened to present WarnerMedia's expansive content universe through innovative technology and inspiring design. I was brought on to create styleframes for how the piece could look and feel based on input content.
