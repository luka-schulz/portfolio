Project · Large-Format Display

HLBMA

For the lobby of HLBMA (Hyundai and LG’s joint battery manufacturing facility) Rare Volume worked with HSAD to design content for a 60-foot LED wall. As the centerpiece of the lobby, and the first thing seen upon entering, the content needed to strike a balance between beauty and elegance. We created a series of backgrounds inspired by nature that keep the space fresh while carrying daily news, weather and brand messaging.

Nature, rendered at scale⁠— four textures across eighteen meters of wall.

Visual direction

An organic visual language

We built an organic visual language around four natural textures: water, stone, grass and wood. Each one is an artful, abstract take on material from the natural world, and each comes in two or three color variations. That gives the client 11 backgrounds in total. The imagery stays calm and tactile so it sits behind messaging without competing with it.

The palette

Four textures, each in two or three colorways: eleven backgrounds, all made to sit quietly behind the messaging.

System / CMS

Designed for change

The wall runs on a custom digital signage CMS, so staff can publish new content on their own without going back to an agency. There are three templates: a main layout that combines messaging, video and a background; an image template that pairs two photos with centered copy; and a full-screen message template for announcements. Lobby staff can pick a template, fill in the fields and publish in a few steps.
