Samsung KX

Digital Signage & Wayfinding

Samsung KX is a massive showcase located in London’s Coal Drops Yard. For the grand opening Rare Volume created an interactive souvenir experience. Using the Galaxy S10, visitors turn their selfies into personalized collages, which are then printed as a custom poster.


Working with the team at Rare Volume, I had the pleasure of animating, and bringing the posters to life as well as editing the case study video.
