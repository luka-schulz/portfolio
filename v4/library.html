03. Library

What follows is a selction of stories that have shaped me and my design's.

Stories—possibly man's greatest invention—have been with us immortal. They have claimed the power to shape our minds, while also being shaped by our minds. Yet, in all their glory they were nomads like the people who told them. When man found a home, so did they.

The physical book gave home to the story and made eternal the power which they held. Libraries became the gardens and graves upon which society was built, and even now, stories have the power to change the world. I am very much the human I am today because of the stories I have met.
